## Pricing Explained

Our pricing is transparent and based on several factors.

### Price Components

1. **Base Tour Price**
   - Per person rate
   - Varies by tour and season

2. **Age Category**
   - Adult: Full price
   - Child: Discounted rate
   - Infant: Free

3. **Group Size**
   - Larger groups may qualify for discounts
   - Check for group rates

4. **Season**
   - Peak season: Higher prices
   - Off-peak: Lower prices

### What's Included in Price

- Transportation
- Guide services
- Meals as specified
- Entrance fees
- Basic travel insurance

### Additional Costs

- Personal expenses
- Tips
- Optional activities
- Special equipment

> **Note:** Prices are per person unless otherwise specified.
