## Leave a Review

Your feedback helps other travelers and improves our service.

### When to Review

- After tour completion
- Within 14 days of tour
- You'll receive an email prompt

### How to Review

1. **Find Your Booking**
   - Go to My Bookings
   - Find completed tour

2. **Click Review**
   - Rate your experience
   - Write your feedback

3. **Submit**
   - Click submit
   - Review appears after moderation

### What to Include

- Overall experience
- Guide performance
- Tour quality
- Any highlights or issues
- Tips for future travelers

### Rating System

- 5 stars: Excellent
- 4 stars: Very good
- 3 stars: Average
- 2 stars: Below average
- 1 star: Poor

> **Tip:** Be honest and specific - your review helps others make informed decisions!
