## Refund Policy

We aim to make cancellations fair for everyone.

### Refund Timeline

| Cancellation Time | Refund |
|-------------------|--------|
| 7+ days before | 100% |
| 3-6 days before | 75% |
| 1-2 days before | 50% |
| Day of tour | No refund |

### Processing Time

- Refunds take 7-14 business days
- Original payment method used
- You'll receive email confirmation

### Non-Refundable Cases

- No-show on tour day
- Partial tour completion
- Violation of tour terms

### Refund Disputes

If you believe you're entitled to a refund but didn't receive one, contact support within 14 days.

> **Note:** Special tours may have different refund terms. Check booking details.
