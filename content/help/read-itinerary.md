## Reading Your Itinerary

Our itineraries are designed to give you a clear picture of your tour day by day.

### Itinerary Sections

#### Day Overview
Each day starts with a brief summary of activities and highlights.

#### Timeline
The detailed schedule shows:
- **Time** - When each activity occurs
- **Activity** - What you'll be doing
- **Duration** - How long each activity takes

#### Meals
Look for meal indicators:
- �Breakfast
- 🍽️ Lunch
- 🍜 Dinner

#### Free Time
Scheduled free time is marked separately so you can explore independently.

> **Tip:** Save your itinerary to your phone for easy reference during the tour.
