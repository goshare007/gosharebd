## Payment Issues

Having trouble with payment? Here's how to resolve common issues.

### Common Issues & Solutions

1. **Card Declined**
   - Check card is not expired
   - Verify sufficient funds
   - Contact your bank

2. **Gateway Error**
   - Try a different payment method
   - Clear browser cache
   - Wait a few minutes and retry

3. **Network Timeout**
   - Check internet connection
   - Try again after a minute

4. **Invalid Card Details**
   - Re-enter card number carefully
   - Check CVV and expiry date

### What to Do

1. Verify all details are correct
2. Try different payment method
3. Contact your bank
4. Reach out to our support team

### Contact Support

If issues persist, contact us with:
- Error message (if any)
- Booking attempt details
- Screenshot of error

> **Note:** Never share your card PIN or OTP with anyone.
