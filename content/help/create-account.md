## Welcome to GoShareBD!

Creating an account is the first step to booking your dream tour. Here's how to do it:

### Step 1: Click Sign Up

1. Visit our homepage and click **Sign Up**
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a strong password

### Step 2: Verify Your Email

Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link.

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile

Add your details:
- Full name
- Phone number
- Date of birth

That's it! You're ready to start exploring tours.

> **Tip:** You can also sign up using your Google account for a quicker process.
