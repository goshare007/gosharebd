## Payment Process

We make payments simple and secure.

### How It Works

1. **Select Tour & Date**
   - Choose your preferred tour
   - Pick your travel date

2. **Enter Traveler Details**
   - Number of guests
   - Special requirements

3. **Review Total**
   - See complete price breakdown
   - Any applicable discounts applied

4. **Payment**
   - Pay securely online
   - Multiple payment options

5. **Confirmation**
   - Instant booking confirmation
   - Email receipt sent

### Payment Security

- SSL encrypted transactions
- Secure payment gateway
- We never store your card details

> **Note:** Payment is required at time of booking to confirm your reservation.
