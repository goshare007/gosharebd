## VAT Explanation

All tour prices include applicable Value Added Tax (VAT).

### What is VAT?

- VAT is a consumption tax
- Applied to goods and services in Bangladesh
- Currently 5% on tour services

### How It's Applied

- VAT is included in the displayed price
- Shown separately in booking summary
- Tax invoice provided on request

### Why We Charge VAT

- Compliance with national tax laws
- Supports government services
- Legitimate business expense

### Tax Invoice

Request a tax invoice by contacting support with your booking reference number.

> **Note:** VAT rates may change based on government regulations.
