## Report Safety Concerns

Your feedback helps us maintain safety standards.

### How to Report

1. **During Tour**
   - Tell your guide immediately
   - They can address most issues

2. **After Tour**
   - Email: support@gosharebd.com
   - Call our office
   - Use contact form

### What to Report

- Unsafe conditions
- Driver/guide behavior
- Vehicle issues
- Activity concerns
- Near-miss incidents
- Any safety violation

### What to Include

- Date and time
- Tour name
- Location
- Description
- Photos if possible
- Your contact details

### Our Response

- Immediate action during tour
- Investigation within 24 hours
- Follow-up with you

### No Retaliation

We welcome all safety feedback. Your reports are confidential and appreciated.

> **Help us improve** by reporting any concerns - big or small.
