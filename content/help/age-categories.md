## Age Categories

We offer different pricing based on age to make tours accessible for everyone.

### Categories

| Category | Age | Pricing |
|----------|-----|---------|
| Adult | 12+ years | Full price |
| Child | 2-11 years | Child rate |
| Infant | Under 2 | Free (lap child) |

### Important Details

- **Adult**: Standard pricing for anyone 12 years and older
- **Child**: Discounted rate includes seat and meals where applicable
- **Infant**: Free when sitting on parent's lap (no separate seat)

### Proof of Age

Please carry age proof for children and infants. Pricing adjustments may be made at check-in if age cannot be verified.

> **Note:** Some tours may have different age policies. Check individual tour pages for specific requirements.
