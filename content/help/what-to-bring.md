## Packing List

Be prepared! Here's what to bring on your tour.

### Essentials

- Valid ID/Passport
- Booking confirmation (print or digital)
- Mobile phone
- Wallet and cards
- Camera

### Clothing

- Comfortable walking shoes
- Light, breathable clothing
- Jacket or sweater (for cold)
- Rain gear
- Hat or cap

### Personal Items

- Sunscreen
- Sunglasses
- Insect repellent
- Personal medications
- Water bottle

### Optional

- Snacks
- Book or entertainment
- Power bank
- Small daypack

> **Tip:** Pack light - you'll be moving around frequently!
