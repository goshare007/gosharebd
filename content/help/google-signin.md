## Quick & Secure Sign-In

Signing in with Google is the fastest way to access your GoShareBD account.

### Benefits of Google Sign-In

- **No password needed** - use your Google credentials
- **Instant access** - skip email verification
- **Security** - Google&apos;s robust security protects your account

### How to Sign In with Google

1. Click **Sign in with Google** on the login page
2. Choose your Google account
3. Grant GoShareBD permission to access your basic info
4. You&apos;re in!

> **Note:** If you previously created an account with the same email, your booking history will be automatically linked.
