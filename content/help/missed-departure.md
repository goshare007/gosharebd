## Missed Departure

Missing the tour departure can be stressful. Here's what to do.

### If You're Running Late

1. **Call Immediately**
   - Contact our emergency number
   - Notify guide if you have their number

2. **Try to Catch Up**
   - Guide may be able to wait briefly
   - Meet at next stop if possible

### Our Policy

- No refund for no-show
- Guide waits max 15 minutes
- After that, tour proceeds

### If Tour Has Left

- Contact our office immediately
- We may offer partial tour credit
- Case-by-case basis

### Prevention Tips

- Save pickup location details
- Set multiple alarms
- Be at pickup 10 minutes early
- Keep guide's contact number

> **Important:** Always be at pickup point at least 15 minutes before departure time.
