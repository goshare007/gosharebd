## Booking for Others

Yes! You can easily book tours for friends, family, or as gifts.

### How to Book for Someone Else

1. During checkout, enter the traveler's details (not yours)
2. Add your contact for emergencies
3. Payment can be made by any method

### Gift Bookings

- Book a tour as a gift for someone special
- The gift recipient will receive the booking details
- They just need to show ID on the tour day

### Important Notes

- The lead traveler must have valid ID
- All communication goes to the booker's email
- For gifts, forward the confirmation to the recipient

> **Tip:** Add a note during booking if this is a special occasion (birthday, anniversary).
