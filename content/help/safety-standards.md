## Safety First

Your safety is our top priority on every tour.

### Our Safety Measures

1. **Verified Partners**
   - Licensed tour operators
   - Regular safety audits
   - Quality checks

2. **Professional Guides**
   - First aid trained
   - Safety certified
   - Experienced with emergencies

3. **Transportation**
   - Licensed vehicles
   - Regular maintenance
   - Insured for passengers

4. **Activities**
   - Safety briefings
   - Proper equipment
   - Age/ability appropriate

### Before the Tour

- Weather checks
- Route planning
- Emergency contacts ready

### During the Tour

- Regular headcounts
- Safety reminders
- First aid available

### Reporting Concerns

If you notice any safety issues, report immediately to your guide or contact us.

> **We're committed** to making every tour safe and enjoyable for everyone.
