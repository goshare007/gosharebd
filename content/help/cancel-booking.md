## Cancel Your Booking

Need to cancel? Here's how to do it properly.

### How to Cancel

1. **Log into Your Account**
   - Go to "My Bookings"
   - Find your booking

2. **Request Cancellation**
   - Click "Cancel Booking"
   - Select cancellation reason

3. **Confirmation**
   - Receive cancellation confirmation
   - Refund process begins

### Cancellation by Phone

Call our support team during business hours to request cancellation.

### What Happens Next

- Confirmation email sent
- Refund processed according to policy
- Any applicable fees deducted

> **Important:** Cancel as early as possible for better refund eligibility.
