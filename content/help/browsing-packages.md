## Find Your Perfect Tour

Our platform offers powerful search and filtering options to help you find the ideal tour package.

### Using the Search Bar

Enter destination names, keywords, or activities to quickly find relevant tours.

### Filter Options

- **Destination** - Filter by location or region
- **Duration** - Choose day trips, weekend getaways, or longer adventures
- **Price Range** - Set your budget
- **Category** - Adventure, cultural, beach, wildlife, etc.
- **Rating** - Filter by customer reviews

### Sorting Results

Sort tours by:
- Price (low to high or high to low)
- Rating
- Popularity
- Duration

> **Tip:** Use multiple filters together to narrow down your options quickly.
