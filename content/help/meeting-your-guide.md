## Your Tour Guide

Our professional guides make your tour memorable.

### Meeting Point

- Look for guide with GoShareBD sign
- Usually at hotel lobby or designated spot
- Check your booking for exact location

### What to Expect

- Warm welcome and introductions
- Brief overview of the day
- Safety instructions
- Group coordination

### How to Identify Your Guide

- Wearing GoShareBD uniform or badge
- Holding a sign with tour name
- Will have your booking list

### Communication

- Guide will share contact number
- Best way to reach during tour
- Group WhatsApp for updates

> **Tip:** Save your guide's number for easy communication during the tour.
