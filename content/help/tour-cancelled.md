## Tour Cancellation by Us

Rarely, we may need to cancel a tour. Here's what happens.

### Why We Cancel

- Insufficient participants
- Safety concerns (weather, conditions)
- Force majeure events
- Operational issues

### Your Options

1. **Full Refund**
   - 100% money back
   - Processed within 7 days

2. **Reschedule**
   - Choose another date
   - No extra charges

3. **Credit**
   - Future booking credit
   - May include bonus

### How We Notify You

- Email notification
- SMS if available
- Phone call if urgent

### We're Sorry

We understand cancellations are frustrating. We work hard to avoid them and appreciate your understanding.

> **Note:** We never cancel tours to profit - only for safety or operational reasons.
