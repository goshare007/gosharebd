## Change Your Tour Date

Need to reschedule? We can help change your tour date.

### How to Request Date Change

1. **Check Availability**
   - Look for your desired new date
   - Confirm spots are available

2. **Submit Request**
   - Go to "My Bookings"
   - Click "Change Date"
   - Select new date

3. **Confirmation**
   - Receive updated booking
   - New confirmation sent

### Terms & Conditions

- Subject to availability
- Price difference may apply
- Request must be 24+ hours before original date
- One date change allowed free

### Price Difference

- If new date is more expensive: Pay difference
- If new date is cheaper: No refund of difference

> **Tip:** Date changes are easier than cancellations - try to reschedule!
