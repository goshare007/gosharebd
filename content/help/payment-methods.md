## Payment Methods

We offer multiple secure payment options.

### Available Methods

- **Credit/Debit Cards**
  - Visa
  - Mastercard
  - American Express

- **Digital Wallets**
  - bKash
  - Nagad
  - Rocket

- **Bank Transfer**
  - Direct bank transfer (local banks)

- **Mobile Banking**
  - Most major banks in Bangladesh

### Currency

- Payments in BDT (Taka)
- Currency conversion handled automatically

### Payment Security

All transactions are encrypted and secure. We comply with PCI DSS standards.

> **Tip:** Some payment methods may offer cashback or discounts - check promotions page!
