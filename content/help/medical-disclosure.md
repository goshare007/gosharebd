## Medical Disclosure

Sharing medical information helps us keep you safe.

### Why It Matters

- Guide can provide better care
- Appropriate activities selected
- Emergency response faster

### What to Disclose

- Heart conditions
- Diabetes
- Epilepsy
- Allergies
- Mobility issues
- Pregnancy
- Any ongoing treatments

### How to Tell Us

1. **During Booking**
   - Add notes in booking
   - Special requirements field

2. **Before Tour**
   - Call our office
   - Email with booking ref
   - Tell guide at start

### What We Do

- Keep information confidential
- Take appropriate precautions
- Have emergency contacts ready

### What to Bring

- Personal medications
- Doctor's note if needed
- Emergency contact info

> **Your privacy** is protected. Medical information is only shared with those who need to know for your safety.
