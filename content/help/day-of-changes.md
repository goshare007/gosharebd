## Same-Day Changes

Sometimes plans change. Here's what to expect.

### Possible Changes

- Weather adjustments
- Road closures or traffic
- Attraction availability
- Time constraints

### How We Handle It

1. **Guide Decision**
   - Guide assesses situation
   - Prioritizes safety and experience

2. **Alternative Activities**
   - Similar activities arranged
   - No additional cost to you

3. **Communication**
   - Guide explains changes
   - Your preferences considered

### Your Options

- Accept the alternative
- Request different option (subject to availability)
- Partial refund for missed activities (rare)

### We're Flexible

Our goal is to give you the best experience possible, even with unexpected changes.

> **Note:** Safety is always our top priority. Sometimes changes are necessary for your wellbeing.
