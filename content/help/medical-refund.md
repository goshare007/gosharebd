## Medical Emergency Refunds

We understand medical emergencies happen. Here's our policy.

### Eligibility

You may qualify for refund if:
- You or a direct family member is ill/injured
- Medical documentation provided
- Cancellation before tour date

### Required Documents

- Medical certificate
- Hospital/doctor letter
- Booking in your name

### Refund Process

1. Submit cancellation with reason
2. Send medical documents within 48 hours
3. We'll review and process refund
4. 100% refund if approved

### What Doesn't Qualify

- Pre-existing conditions not disclosed
- Minor illness without documentation
- Cancellation after tour start

### Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend travel insurance for:
- Medical emergencies
- Trip cancellations
- Unexpected events

> **Important:** Provide accurate medical documentation for faster processing.
