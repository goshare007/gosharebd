## Manage Your Reviews

Changed your mind? Here's how to edit or delete your review.

### Editing Your Review

1. **Find Your Review**
   - Go to My Account
   - Click My Reviews

2. **Edit**
   - Click edit button
   - Make changes

3. **Save**
   - Submit changes
   - Review updated

### Deleting Your Review

1. **Request Deletion**
   - Contact support
   - Provide booking reference

2. **Confirmation**
   - We'll confirm deletion
   - Review removed

### What Can Be Edited

- Rating
- Review text
- Add photos

### When Editing Isn't Allowed

- After 30 days
- If under investigation
- Legal reasons

### Our Policy

- First edit is free
- Multiple edits may need review
- Deletion is permanent

> **Note:** Edited reviews go through moderation again.
