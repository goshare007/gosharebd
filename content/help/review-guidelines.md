## Review Guidelines

We maintain high-quality reviews to help travelers make informed decisions.

### What We Welcome

- Honest experiences
- Specific details
- Useful tips
- Balanced feedback
- Photos (when appropriate)

### What We Don't Allow

- Profanity or offensive language
- Personal attacks
- False information
- Spam or promotional content
- Private information about others
- Competitor mentions

### Moderation Process

All reviews go through:
1. Automated spam check
2. Content review
3. Publication

This takes 24-48 hours.

### Your Rights

- Fair and impartial review
- No removal for negative feedback
- Right to respond to responses
- Privacy protected

### Reporting Fake Reviews

If you see suspicious reviews:
- Report to support
- We'll investigate
- Take appropriate action

### Tips for Great Reviews

- Be specific about what was good/bad
- Mention the guide by name
- Note time of year
- Include practical tips
- Add helpful photos

> **Thank you** for helping our community with your honest reviews!
