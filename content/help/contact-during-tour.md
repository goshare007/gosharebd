## Get in Touch

We're here to help during your tour too!

### How to Reach Us

1. **Through Your Guide**
   - Guide is your first contact
   - Can handle most issues

2. **Emergency Hotline**
   - Available during tour hours
   - 24/7 for urgent matters

3. **WhatsApp**
   - Quick text-based communication
   - Group chat with other travelers

### What We Can Help With

- Lost items
- Medical emergencies
- Itinerary questions
- Special requests
- Complaints or concerns

### What to Provide

- Your booking reference
- Your location
- Brief description of issue

### Response Time

- Urgent: Immediate
- General: Within 30 minutes

> **Tip:** Save our contact number before the tour starts!
