## What's Included?

Each tour package clearly lists what's included to help you plan accordingly.

### Common Inclusions

- **Transportation** - Pickup and drop-off from designated points
- **Meals** - Breakfast, lunch, dinner as specified
- **Guide Services** - Professional tour guide throughout the trip
- **Entrance Fees** - All attraction tickets
- **Accommodation** - For multi-day tours

### What's Not Included

- Personal expenses
- Travel insurance
- Tips and gratuities
- Optional activities
- Alcoholic beverages

> **Important:** Always check the detailed itinerary for exact inclusions and exclusions.
