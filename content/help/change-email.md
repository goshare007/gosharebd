## Change Your Email

Need to change your email address? Here's how.

### Before You Start

- Have access to both old and new email
- Know your account password
- Update payment methods if needed

### How to Change

1. **Go to Settings**
   - My Account
   - Security/Email

2. **Request Change**
   - Enter new email address
   - Confirm password

3. **Verify New Email**
   - Check new inbox
   - Click confirmation link

4. **Complete Process**
   - Log in with new email
   - Old email no longer works

### What Happens

- Booking confirmations go to new email
- Password stays the same
- All history preserved

### Problems?

If you can't access old email:
- Contact support with ID verification
- Additional verification needed

> **Important:** Update email before upcoming bookings to receive confirmations.
