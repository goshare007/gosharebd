## Update Your Profile

Keep your account information up to date.

### What You Can Update

- Full name
- Phone number
- Date of birth
- Profile photo
- Address

### How to Update

1. **Log into Your Account**
   - Go to My Account
   - Click Profile

2. **Edit Information**
   - Click field to edit
   - Make changes

3. **Save Changes**
   - Click Save
   - Confirmation shown

### Important Notes

- Name must match ID for tours
- Phone needed for tour updates
- Email changes require verification

### Profile Photo

- Upload a clear photo
- Helps guides identify you
- Optional but recommended

> **Tip:** Check your profile before each booking to ensure information is current.
