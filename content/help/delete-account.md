## Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's how to delete your account.

### What Happens When Deleted

- Account permanently removed
- Booking history deleted
- Cannot recover account
- Must create new account to book again

### Before You Delete

- Cancel any upcoming bookings
- Download booking history
- Use any account credits

### How to Delete

1. **Go to Settings**
   - My Account
   - Delete Account

2. **Confirm Decision**
   - Read implications
   - Enter password

3. **Final Confirmation**
   - Confirm via email
   - Account deleted

### What We Keep

- Financial records (by law)
- Anonymous feedback

### Having Issues?

If you can't delete:
- Contact support
- May need ID verification

### Rejoining

After deletion:
- Create new account anytime
- Past history not recoverable

> **Think carefully** - deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
