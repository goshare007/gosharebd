## Your Booking History

All your bookings are saved in your account.

### How to View

1. **Go to My Bookings**
   - Click My Bookings in menu
   - See all bookings

2. **View Details**
   - Click on any booking
   - Full itinerary shown

### What's Available

- Past bookings
- Upcoming bookings
- Cancelled bookings
- Booking details
- E-tickets
- Invoices

### Search & Filter

- By date
- By status
- By tour name

### Download Documents

- E-tickets
- Invoices
- Confirmation emails

### Need Past Bookings?

If you can't find old bookings:
- Check email confirmations
- Contact support with details

> **Tip:** Bookmark your important bookings for quick access!
