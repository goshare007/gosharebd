## Traveling Together

Booking for a group? Here's how to add all your travel companions.

### Adding Group Members

1. During booking, enter the number of travelers
2. Fill in details for each person:
   - Full name
   - Age category (Adult/Child/Infant)
   - Special requirements

### Group Bookings

- Minimum 2 travelers for group rates
- One person acts as the lead traveler
- All members receive confirmation details

### Important Notes

- All travelers must agree to terms and conditions
- Each person needs valid identification
- Dietary requirements should be specified

> **Tip:** For large groups (10+), contact us directly for special arrangements.
