## Family-Friendly Tours

We welcome families with children of all ages!

### Booking for Children

- Children aged 2-11 qualify for child discounts
- Child pricing includes most inclusions (meals, activities)
- Most tours are child-friendly

### Booking for Infants

- Infants under 2 travel free on parent's lap
- No booking needed for infants, but please inform us
- Limited services available for infants

### What to Bring

- Child safety seat (if needed)
- Stroller (check tour suitability)
- Baby food and diapers
- Any medications

### Safety Considerations

- Some activities may have age restrictions
- Our guides are trained to handle family groups
- Rest stops are provided on longer tours

> **Tip:** Contact us in advance for special arrangements for families with young children.
