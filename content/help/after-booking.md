## After Booking

Here's what happens once you complete your booking:

### Immediate Steps

1. **Confirmation Email**
   - Sent within minutes of payment
   - Contains booking reference number
   - Includes e-ticket and itinerary

2. **Booking Status**
   - Check "My Bookings" in your account
   - View all booking details

### Before Your Tour

- **24 hours before**: Reminder with pickup details
- **Day before**: Final confirmation call
- **Day of tour**: Pickup at designated time and place

### What You'll Receive

- E-ticket (print or digital)
- Detailed itinerary
- Emergency contact numbers
- List of what to bring

> **Important:** Keep your booking confirmation handy until the tour is complete.
